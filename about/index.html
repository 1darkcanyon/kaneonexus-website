Kaneon Edward Parker

Founder and CEO of Integrated Intelligence Solutions. Builder of NEXUS at kaneonexus.net, formerly kaneonexus.com.

I'm Kaneon Parker, though most people call me Canyon. I live in Josephine County, Oregon, near Grants Pass. I build software, I prospect for gold, and I knap obsidian. This page is my story, told plainly.

How I learned to build

I didn't come up through a traditional path. When AI arrived, I could have let it pass me by. Instead I chose to learn alongside it, using it as a collaborator, a tutor, and a sounding board. Everything I've built came from asking questions, testing ideas, breaking things, and fixing them with AI as a mirror. It reflected my own thinking back to me, and along the way it helped me learn emotional intelligence as well as code. I set the vision and make the decisions, and AI helps me turn them into working software.

How I build

I'm the only developer, and I write, test, and ship everything from my phone. I use Termux for code, GitHub Actions for cloud builds, a Samsung Galaxy A36 as my main device, and a rugged CAT S62 for field testing. I don't have a studio or a team. That setup has limits, and I'd rather you know them than guess.

What I've made

NEXUS is the center of it. Around it are apps and experiments: ParticleFlow for sacred geometry visuals, ParaNexus for AR paranormal detection, LUNA-TIC for lunar and emotional forecasting, a security camera hub, and accessibility tools. I also run a multi-AI workflow I call the Council of Minds, where several AI systems work together on the same projects. Some of this is finished and live. Some is still in progress. I'll always tell you which is which.

Beyond the screen

I'm a gold prospector and field researcher. I spend real time in the Josephine County hills, studying the geology and digging through old mining records. I also build custom instruments and boomerangs, and I produce music.

My writing

I'm the author of the Nexus Translation of Truth, a ten-book cosmological work. Volume one is published. It's my own exploration of how reality fits together, not settled science. Some of it is unconventional, so question it, challenge it, and tell me where you disagree.

Why I'm sharing this

I started building because I needed tools that didn't exist yet, or that needed to be better. For three years I've lived off-grid, and I launched my company from that home as its foundation. Now I've been told that home isn't mine, and I'm facing losing it. I'm not hiding that, because I believe I deserve to be known. I build what I know will have a positive impact, and I build what I need as I go through life, physically, metaphorically, and digitally.

The Obsidian Dreamwalker

Some people know me by that name. I knap obsidian by hand, I've given away many pieces, and I'll keep making more.

Intelligence beyond the artificial

Intelligence, to me, isn't only artificial. Emotional intelligence matters just as much: understanding what I feel, understanding what others feel, and responding with care. Learning it has been as important to my work as learning to code. The word "Intelligence" in Integrated Intelligence Solutions covers both.

Integrated and with integrity

I face everything by integrating it and with integrity. I bring the code, the creek, the stone, and the story together into one life and one body of work. And I stay honest about the wins, the setbacks, and the hard parts I'm in the middle of right now. That's what the name Integrated Intelligence Solutions means to me.
