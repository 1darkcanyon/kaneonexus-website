What LUNA-TIC 4.0 does

Enter your name and birth date. LUNA-TIC gives you a daily read that blends the lunar cycle with your numbers and a few cards.

Moon phase

Real phase, percent lit and moon age for today, plus a 7-day lunar forecast.

Numerology

Your life path number, worked out from your birth date, with its meaning.

Daily draws

A tarot card, a rune and a playing card from full decks. Your draws stay the same all day.

Nexus message

A short spiritual note from the Nexus AI presence to end the reading.

Runs on your phone

Works fully offline in Termux. A wake-lock script keeps it running while your screen is off.

Your own profile

Create a profile and log in. Passwords are hashed, and several profiles can share one device.

Get LUNA-TIC 4.0

The source and builds live on GitHub. To run the Python build in Termux:

pkg update && pkg install python
pip install -r requirements.txt
chmod +x termux_wake_lock.sh
./termux_wake_lock.sh

Then open http://127.0.0.1:5000 in your browser.
